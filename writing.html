My mother taught me to write.

I vividly remember an 8th grade assignment for which I had to write a newsletter ... on a manual typewriter. The section about the weather read: "Whether or not there will be any weather depends on whether or not the weatherman predicts any weather." Writing has always been part of my toolkit, throughout my career. I'm showing my most recent Substack posts here and I invite you to read and subscribe to my feed. Some of these are very recent. I also saved a few evergreen pieces from my now deprecated business website and republished them on Substack. Read. Enjoy. Disagree. Comment.
