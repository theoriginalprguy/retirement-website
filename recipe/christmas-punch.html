Christmas Punch

Miscellaneous

Typed from the card

Christmas Punch 2 cups boiling water 3/4 cups sugar 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon 1 (46 oz) can pineapple juice, chilled 1 (32 oz) bottle cranberry juice, chilled 1 (28 oz) bottle gingerale, chilled Combine water, sugar & cinnamon in a saucepan; bring to a boil, and stir until sugar dissolves. Chill. Combine sugar syrup, fruit juice, & ginger ale. Serve over ice. Yield about 3-3/4 quarts.
