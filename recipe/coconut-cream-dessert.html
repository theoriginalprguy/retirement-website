Coconut Cream Dessert

Desserts

Typed from the card

Cocoanut Cream Dessert 60 Ritz crackers (crushed) 1/4 lb. butter, melted 1-1/2 cups milk 1/2 gal vanilla ice cream, softened 2 sm pkg cocoanut cream instant pudding 1 9 oz cool whip (["use about" written above] 1/2 of it) Crush crackers reserve some for topping (about 1 cup) Melt butter & mix with crackers Press on bottom only of 9x13 pan Back of cardPour milk into softened ice cream beat with mixer until smooth. Then add instant pudding and beat again. Pour over crackers & let stand in refrigerator until firm. Spread with cool whip & sprinkle with reserved crumbs. Mary Jane Aumont's receipe
