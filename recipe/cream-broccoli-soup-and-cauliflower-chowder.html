Cream Broccoli Soup and Cauliflower Chowder

Soups and Stews

Typed from the card

CREAM OF BROCCOLI SOUP 2 T butter or oleo 1/4 cup chopped onion stem chopped celery 1-1/2 lbs. fresh broccolio 2 cups beef boullion 3/4 cup light cream 2 T chopped scallions (optional) Melt butter in medium sauce pan. Add onion and celery and cook until tender. Cut up broccoli into large sauce pan. Put boullion with broccoli. Cook about 20 minutes or until broccoli is soft. Add cream and cook about 2 minutes or until thickened. Decorate with scallions. CAULIFLOWER CHOWDER 1/4 cup chopped onion 1-1/4 cup water 10 oz. frozen or head fresh cauliflower 1/4 cup chopped or sliced mushrooms 3/4 cup low fat milk 4 tsp cornstarth 1 cup or 4 oz. shredded American cheese Cook onion in water until tender. Add cauliflower and mushrooms. Bring to boil, reduce heat, cover and simmer about 8 minutes (until cauliflower is tender). Combine milk and cornstarch and mix well. Stir into hot vegetable mixture. Cook and stir over medium heat until thick and bubbly. Then stir in cheese until melted. Serve
