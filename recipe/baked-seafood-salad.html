Baked Seafood Salad

BAKED SEAFOOD SALAD 1 (10 or 12 oz.) package frozen peeled and deveined shrimp 1 (6-1/2 oz.) can crabmeat, flaked and picked over 1-1/2 cups finely chopped celery 1/2 cup finely chopped green pepper 1/4 cup finely chopped onion 1 cup mayonnaise 1 teaspoon Worchestershire sauce 1/2 teaspoon salt 1-1/2 cups crushed potato chips 1/2 teaspoon paprika 2 tablespoons butter or margarine Drop shrimp into boiling salted water and Back of cardcook until pink (about one minute after water returns to boil. If shrimps are large, cut into half-inch pieces. Combine with crab, celery, pepper and onion. Mix mayonnaise with Worchestershire sauce and salt and fold in. Spread in buttered two-quart baking dish or into 6 one-cup casseroles. Bake in 400 degree (hot) oven ten minutes. Blend potato chips with paprika and sprinkle over mixture. Dot with butter. Bake ten minutes more or until potato chips are browned. Makes six servings.
