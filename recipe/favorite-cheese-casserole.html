FAVORITE CHEESE CASSEROLE Betty Crocker recipes Arrange in greased baking dish: 6 slices bread Cover with: 1/2 lb. sharp cheese (6 slices, 1/8-in. thick) Top with: 6 more slices bread to make "sandwiches" Pour over cheese and bread a mixture of: 4 egg yolks, beaten (or 2 whole eggs), 2-1/2 cups milk, 1 tsp. salt, 1/8 tsp. pepper, 1/4 tsp. dry mustard Chill in refrigerator so milk mixture will soften bread. Bake about 1 hour in moderate oven (350°). Serves 6 to 8. 1704-11-11-47 *A registered trade mark of General Mills, Inc. (OVER)
