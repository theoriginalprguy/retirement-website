Graham Cracker Pie Filling

Desserts: Pies and Pastries

Typed from the card

GRAHAM CRACKER PIE FILLING 2 cups milk Scald milk 1/3 cup flour ) 3/4 cups sugar ) Combine these 3 ingredients 1/4 tsp. salt ) 2 egg yolks 1 tsp. vanilla Combine flour-sugar-["milk" crossed out, "salt" written above] mixture with hot milk. Cook about [typed 20, changed in pen to 10] minutes in double boiler. Add hot milk mixture to slightly beaten egg yolks. Return to heat and cook about 5 minutes more. Cool, then add vanilla. Put into graham cracker crust. Filling for one pie. When doubling receipe, add a bit more flour. Back of card (handwritten)Use eggs whites for merangue. Add a bit of sugar after whites are beaten almost stiff. Continue beating. Spread on top of pie filling & sprinkle on a few graham cracker crumbs. Brown in oven or under broiler. If broiling watch carefully. Can burn quickly.
