Fudge

Candy

Typed from the card

FUDGE 2 pkgs. chocolate pudding (3-3/4 oz. size) (regular pudding - do not use instant) 1/2 cup milk 1 - 1-lb. box 10-X sugar 1 tsp vanilla 1/2 cup or 1 quarter butter or margarine Melt margarine, add chocolate pudding and milk, bring to a boil. Boil for one minute. Add this mixture to 10-X sugar and mix well. Add vanilla & mix again. Pour into greased pan or pan lined with foil wrap. Chill until firm & then cut into squares. (If desired, add 2 tbl. marshmallow or 2 tbl. Back of cardpeanut butter or chopped nuts or cocoanut before putting into pan.)
