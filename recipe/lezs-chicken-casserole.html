Lez's Chicken Casserole

Casseroles

Typed from the card

Lez's Chicken Casserole 5 chicken breasts, boned, skinned & split 1 can cream of chicken soup 1 pint sour cream 1 - 4 oz. package cream cheese 5 strips bacon 1 pkg sliced pressed beef (optional) Wash chicken, pat dry. Cut bacon strips in half - place strips on underside of chicken bringing ends up over top side. If using the pressed beef, lay pieces flat in bottom of rectangular baking dish - place chicken on top. Cut cream cheese into 12 equal squares, use 10 of the squares, placing Back of cardone square on top of each breast. Combine cream of chicken soup and pint of sour cream. Pour over top. Place dish, tightly covered with aluminum foil, into 325° oven and bake for 1 hr. & 45 min., basting occasionally. Remove foil and bake an additional 15 minutes.
