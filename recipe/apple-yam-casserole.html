Apple Yam Casserole

Vegetables

Typed from the card

APPLE--YAM CASSEROLE 2 cups canned apple slices, drained 3/4 cup orange juice 1/2 cup brown sugar 1/4 teaspoon salt 1/2 teaspoon mace 1/4 teaspoon ginger 1/2 cup chopped pecans 2 cans (1 lb. each) yams, drained, cut into 1/2 inch slices 2 tablespoons butter or margarine Combine apple slices, orange juice, brown sugar, salt, mace and ginger in a small Back of cardsaucepan. Simmer for 5 minutes. Stir in pecans. In a greased 1-1/2 qt. casserole place alternate layers of sliced yams and apple mixture. Dot with butter. Cover and bake in a 350 degrees oven for 30 minutes, or until completely heated. Makes 8 servings.
