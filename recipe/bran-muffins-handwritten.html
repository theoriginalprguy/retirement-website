Bran Muffins (Handwritten)

Biscuits and Bread

Typed from the card

Bran Muffins Mix & let stand until cool 2 C boiling water 2 C Nabisco 100% Bran Then cream 1 heaping cup Crisco 3 C sugar 4 eggs 1 qt buttermilk 5 C flour Back of card5 tsp. baking soda 4 C Kellogg's 100% All Bran 2 tsp. salt Add all this to 1st cooled mixture. Spoon into muffin tins 2/3 full. Bake at 375-400° for 20 min. Store in covered container in refrigerator. Do not stir when using again. Stores for 6 weeks. (Can make 1/2 of recipe)
