New Years Day Bean Soup

Soups and Stews

Typed from the card

NEW YEAR'S DAY BEAN SOUP Wash beans and place in a large kettle. Cover with water 2" -- add 2 TB salt. Soak overnight. Drain in the morning. Add 2 quarts water and ham hocks or ham. Bring to a boil. Simmer slowly for 3 hours. Add 1 large chopped onion, one #2-1/2 can of tomatoes. Season with 1 tsp chili powder, juice of lemon, salt and pepper, if desired. Remove ham hocks, cut up ham, and add pieces to the above. Simmer slowly one hour or more. Serve with tossed salad and crackers.
