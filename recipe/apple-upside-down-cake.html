Apple Upside Down Cake

Desserts

Typed from the card

APPLE UPSIDE-DOWN CAKE 2 tart red apples 1/4 cup butter or margerine 1/2 cup honey 1/2 cup broken English walnuts 1/3 cup chopped maraschino cherries 1 pkg. spice cake mix Core unpeeled apples and slice in rings 1/4 in. thick. Melt butter in an oven-going 10-in. skillet. Add honey and the apple rings; cook 3 minutes, turning once. Sprinkle with nuts and maraschino cherries. Prepare cake mix batter as directed on package. Back of cardPour 2 cups batter over apples. (Bake remaining batter as cup cakes) Bake cake in moderate oven (350°) 30 to 35 min. or until done. (Bake the cupcakes 20 to 25 mins.) Cool upside-down cake 5 min. in pan. Turn upsidedown on serving plate. Serve warm. Top servings with whipped cream or ice cream.
