Busy Day Lemon Cake

Desserts: Pies and Pastries

Typed from the card

BUSY DAY LEMON CAKE 1 large (8 oz.) package Borden's cream cheese 2 cups whole milk 1 package Jell-O Lemon Instant Pudding 9-inch graham cracker crust Stir cream cheese until very soft; blend in 1/2 cup milk. Add remaining milk and the pudding mix. Beat slowly with egg beater just until well mixed, about 1 minute. (Do not overbeat.) Pour at once into graham cracker crust. Sprinkle graham cracker crumbs lightly over top. Chill about an hour. Serves 8.
