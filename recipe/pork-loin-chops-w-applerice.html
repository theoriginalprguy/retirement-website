Pork Loin Chops w AppleRice

Entrees: Pork

Typed from the card

[Newspaper clipping; the article text before the recipe is not transcribed.] WITH APPLE RICE 8 loin pork chops, 1/2 to 3/4 inch thick (about 2-1/2 pounds) Salt and pepper 1 tbsp. shortening 1 pkg (7 oz.) chicken-flavored rice mix 1 med. cooking apple, peeled and diced (about 1 cup) 1-3/4 cups water 2 tbsp. butter Trim excess fat from pork chops; season with salt and pepper. Brown quickly in shortening in a large skillet. Drain on absorbent paper. Combine rice mix and contents of seasoning packet, apple, and water in a lightly greased shallow 2-quart casserole. Mix well to moisten all rice. Dot with butter. Arrange pork chops on top of rice mixture. Cover and bake at 350 degrees for about 45 minutes or until chops are tender and well done. Makes 4 to 6 servings. Note: If a flameproof casserole is available, brown chops in the casserole; then continue as directed.
