Impossible French Apple Pie and Jello Dessert

Desserts

Typed from the card

[Printed on Elizabeth W. Kirchner's letterhead.] IMPOSSIBLE FRENCH APPLE PIE 6 cups sliced apples 1-1/4 tsp. cinnamon 1/4 tsp. nutmeg 1 cup sugar 3/4 cup milk 1/2 cup Bisquick 2 eggs 2 tbsp. butter or margerine Grease 10-inch pie plate. Fill with apples. Beat remaining ingredients in blender and pour on the apples. Then sprinkle with STREUSEL TOPPING 1 cup Bisquick 1/2 cup nuts 1/3 cup brown sugar 3 tbsp. butter Bake the pie at 325 degrees for 60 minutes. JELLO DESSERT 1 - 6 oz. box raspberry jello 1 - 20 oz. can crushed pineapple 1 - 15 oz. can blueberries in heavy syrup (never use frozen berries) 1/2 pint whipping cream Dissolve jello in 1 cup boiling water, blend in pineapple and blueberries including all the juice from both cans. Whip the cream and blend into the first mixture. Refrigerate until ready to serve. Makes 8 to 10 generous servings
