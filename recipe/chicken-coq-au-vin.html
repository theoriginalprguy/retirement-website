Chicken Coq au Vin

Entrees: Poultry

Typed from the card

CHICKEN COQUABAN ["COQUE AU VIN" written above] (D. HOGARTH) 3 SPLIT, SKINNED, BONED CHICKEN BREASTS - SALT well Mix 1 cup SOUR CREAM, 1 CAN CREAM OF MUSHROOM SOUP, 1 CAN MUSHROOM PIECES, 1 CAN whole MUSHROOMS, 1/2 cup COOKING SHERRY. POUR OVER CHICKEN and BAKE AT 350° FOR 1 hr & 15 MIN. SPRINKLE WITH PAPRIKA. For 8 BREASTS double receipe. Good SERVED with RICE.

Typed from the original, keeping its spelling and wording.
