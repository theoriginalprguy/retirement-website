Typed from the card

CHILE CON CARNE 1 lb. ground beef - season to taste medium onion ) adjust amounts to your small green pepper ) own taste Slice onion & green pepper (slivers) into small amount of vegetable oil in a large frying pan. Cook a few minutes, then add the meat and brown same. (I like to break the meat into bite size pieces. They break up some as you cook & stir anyway) After meat has browned, add 2 cans Joan of Arc kidney beans and 2 cans tomato soup Rinse out soup can with about 1/2 filled with water which you add to the other ingredients. (Poor sentence structure!) (over) Back of cardAdd chili power to taste & a little celery salt. Mix everything thoroughly, cover and cook over medium heat. When heated well, turn to low heat and let cook an hour or so. Stir occasionally and add water if needed. This is a very thick chile. I usually serve on dinner plates ["& eat with fork" crossed out]. I serve it with mashed potatoes. We like the chile on top of the potatoes. If you like thinner chile to serve in bowl ["with spoon" crossed out], just add more water while cooking and a little more salt. I usually make a larger quantity & freeze for later use.
