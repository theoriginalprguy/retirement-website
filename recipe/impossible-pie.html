Impossible Pie

Desserts: Pies and Pastries

Typed from the card

IMPOSSIBLE PIE 4 eggs 1/2 cup oleo 3/4 cup sugar 1 tsp vanilla 2 cups milk 1/2 cup bisquick Put all ingredients in the blender & mix for a few minutes. Pour into a greased 10" pie pan. Bake at 350° for 45 minutes. Variations: Add 3/4 cup pumpkin or 1/2 cup lemon juice & 1 tsp. lemon rind Topping: 1/2 cup cocoanut or 1/2 cup nuts or sprinkle with cinnamon
