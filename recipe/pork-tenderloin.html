Pork Tenderloin

Entrees: Pork

Typed from the card

Pork Tenderloin with Mustard Sauce 2 tenderloins (1-1/2 lb each) 1/4 c soy sauce 1/4 c bourbon 2 tbsp brown sugar Mix marinade & put tenderloins and marinade in plastic bag. Leave overnight or from morning to evening Bake covered at 325° for about 1 hour with some of the sauce in bottom of pan - Uncover for last 15 minutes to brown. Slice thin and serve with following sauce: 2/3 c sour cream 2/3 c. mayonnaise 2 tbsp dry mustard 3-4 green onions, finely chopped Mix above ingredients & keep in refrig. for several hours to blend flavors Back of card(Actually you can easily halve the recipe for the sauce and have plenty. It goes a long way)
