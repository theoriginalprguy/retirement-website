Chewy Oatmeal Cookies

Cakes and Cookies

Typed from the card

[In pencil:] Chewy OATMEAL COOKIES (Nan Wiley's receipe) 1 cup brown sugar 1 teaspoon soda 1 cup white sugar 1 cup flour 1 cup shortening 1 cup raisins ) ground 2 eggs 1 cup dates ) together 1 teaspoon vanilla 1 cup chopped pecans 1/2 teaspoon salt 1 cup cocoanut 4 cups oatmeal Cream together both sugars and shortening. Add beaten eggs and vanilla. Sift salt, soda and flour together. Add to creamed mixture. Then add oatmeal and ground fruits plus nuts and cocoanut. Mix well. Back of cardDrop by teaspoonful onto greased cookie sheets. Bake about 10 minutes at 325-350 degrees. The cookies fall some after leaving the oven.
