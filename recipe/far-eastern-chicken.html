Far Eastern Chicken

Entrees: Poultry

Typed from the card

Here's what's cookin': Far Eastern Chicken Recipe from the kitchen of: M. Bechtold 2-1/2 - 3 cups cubed cooked chicken 2-1/2 cups rice krispies or toasted bread crumbs 1 small can crushed pineapple 1 can drained bean sprouts 1 can mushrooms (small) 1 teas. salt Combine above & place in casserole 1/4 cup soya sauce 1/4 " brown sugar 3/4 " pineapple juice 2 T cornstarch 2 T vinegar (over) Back of cardPut soya sauce, brown sugar, pineapple juice and cornstarch in sauce pan & bring to a boil. Boil 2 minutes to thicken. Take off heat & add vinegar. Pour over first mixture. Bake 350° for 25-30 minutes for cubed chicken & longer for pieces of chicken.
