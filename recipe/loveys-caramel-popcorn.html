Lovey's Caramel Popcorn

Miscellaneous

Typed from the card

Lovey's Caramel popcorn Back of card5 qts p[opcorn] ["two" written in pen above] 1/2 cups popcorn kernels 2 cups lt brown sugar 1/2 lb butter (1 cup) 1/2 cup corn syrup (light) Bring to boil, Boil 5 mins. Remove from fire & add 1 ["tsp" crossed out] scant 1/2 tsp baking soda & Beat good. Pour over pop corn. Put in 250° oven in big deep pan & stir every 15 mins (1 hr total)
