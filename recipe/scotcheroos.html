Here's what's cookin': Scotcheroos Recipe from the kitchen of: J. Hobday 1 cp. sugar 1 cp. light corn syrup 1 cp. choc. bits (6 oz) 1 cp. butterscotch bits (6 oz) 1 cp. peanut butter 6 cp. rice krispies Combine sugar & syrup. Cook over moderate heat, stir frequently until mix begins to bubble. Remove from heat. Stir in peanut butter. Mix well. Add rice krispies. Stir til well blended. Press in buttered 13 x 9 pan. Melt chocolate & butterscotch together over very low heat, stir til blended. Remove from heat, spread over rice krispies. Cool til firm. Cut in bars.
