Sea Food Special

Entrees: Seafood

Typed from the card

SEA FOOD SPECIAL 1 can (2 ozs.) sliced mushrooms, drained 1/2 cup chopped onion 2 tablespoons butter or margarine 1 can (10 ozs.) frozen condensed cream of shrimp soup 1/2 cup milk 2 cups cut-up cooked seafood (white fish, lobster, or shrimp) 1 tablespoon chopped parsley 1 tablespoon sherry (optional) 1 teaspoon lemon juice Patty shells or toast cups In saucepan, cook mushrooms and onion in Back of cardbutter until onion is tender. Add soup and milk. Heat until soup thaws, stirring now and then. Add fish, parsley, sherry, and lemon juice. Heat, stirring now and then. Serve in patty shells, toast cups, or on rice. Makes 4 servings.
