Desert life

Field notes in color. Rooted here, wandering wide.

Photos from the Sonoran Desert and beyond.

A visual field journal of desert plants, wildlife, and nature observations from Phoenix, Arizona and the Sonoran Desert. Species include saguaro cactus, palo verde, agave, mesquite, desert wildflowers, Gambel's quail, cactus wren, curve-billed thrasher, and many other natives of the Sonoran Desert ecosystem. All observations logged on iNaturalist by David Kirk, Docent at Desert Botanical Garden.
